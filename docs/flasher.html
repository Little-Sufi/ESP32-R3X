Web Serial is not supported in this browser. Serial access denied or blocked. Download .bin
Browser Notice: Web Serial requires Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Opera on desktop. Safari, Firefox, and mobile browsers do not support Web Serial.
STEP 01
Connect USB-C
Plug your ESP32-S3 board into your computer with a data-capable USB-C cable.
STEP 02
Bootloader Mode
If not detected: Hold BOOT, press & release RESET, then release BOOT.
STEP 03
Click & Install
Click the blue button above, pick the COM port from the prompt, and select Install.
STEP 04
Reboot Board
Once flashing finishes, press the physical RESET button on the board to boot into the UI!
